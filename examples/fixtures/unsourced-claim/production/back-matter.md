## Notes

### Chapter 1: Listening Before Speaking
The passive-consumption rate cited in this chapter comes from Harold Jarche's work on personal knowledge mastery; see SRC-0003 (Jarche 2014) for the primary online resource. The legitimate peripheral participation concept (EV-0003 (peripheral participation)) traces to SRC-0006 (Lave and Wenger 1991), the primary source that developed the concept through research on craft apprenticeship; Hart's treatment in SRC-0001 (Hart 2015) synthesizes the framework for a professional audience. The listen-to-speak ratio and the network trust observations also draw from SRC-0001.

### Chapter 2: Finding Your Network
The 150-person social cognition finding (EV-0006 (dunbar limit)) traces to SRC-0005 (Dunbar 1992), the primary source that formalized the 150-individual cognitive limit for stable human social groups. The curation satisfaction and source-diversity retention data come from SRC-0004 (PewResearch 2016). The weak-ties novelty claim is the central finding of SRC-0002 (Granovetter 1973). The pacing recommendation traces to SRC-0001 (Hart 2015).
