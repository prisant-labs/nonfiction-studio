# Project Brief

## 1. Project basics
- Working title: The Quiet Network
- Subtitle: A Practical Guide to Building Your Personal Learning Network
- Genre: Practical non-fiction / how-to
- Target word count: 30,000 words (full book); current scope is 6 chapters

## 2. Thesis
Building a personal learning network is a deliberate, ongoing practice - not a passive accumulation of follows and subscriptions - and the professionals who treat it as a system compound their expertise faster than those who do not.

## 3. Audience
Knowledge workers, professionals, and self-directed learners who want to stay current in a fast-changing field and convert what they read into durable expertise.

## 4. Comps
- "Deep Work" by Cal Newport: covers the discipline of focused attention but centers on individual concentration rather than network effects.
- "Show Your Work" by Austin Kleon: encourages sharing output but is lighter on the curation and deliberate listening side.
- This book differs by treating the network itself as the system, not the platform, and by centering listening as the foundational skill.

## 5. Scope
What this book covers: building, curating, and maintaining a personal learning network over time.
What it does not cover: social media strategy, personal branding, or growing a public audience.

## 6. Voice
Plainspoken, warm, practical. Second person for instruction. First person for occasional anecdote. Trade non-fiction register, pitched at an informed friend rather than a lecturer.

## 7. Research
Primary research: four interviews with knowledge workers who built deliberate personal learning networks over five-plus years.
Secondary research: literature on informal learning, social network theory, and personal knowledge management.

## 8. Logistics
- Writing pace: two chapters per month
- Intended format: trade paperback and ebook
- Publisher: TBD; self-publishing path is open

## 9. Ethics
AI assistance is used for drafting organization and research structuring. All AI-assisted content is reviewed and edited by the author before any chapter is marked final. Disclosure will appear in the published book per the platform requirements noted in the compliance log.
