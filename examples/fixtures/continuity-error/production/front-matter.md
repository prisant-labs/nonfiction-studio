## Preface / Author's note
This book began as a note to myself. I had been reading voraciously for years but could not always say what I had learned. The reading felt productive. The retention was thinner than I expected. What I had built was not a learning network; it was a consumption habit with better aesthetics.

The practices in this book are the ones I found my way to by trial and error, and later confirmed against the research on how informal learning actually works. They are not complicated. But they require the kind of deliberate attention that does not come naturally in an environment engineered to keep you scrolling.

I hope you find them useful.
