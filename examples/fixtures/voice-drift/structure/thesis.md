# Thesis

## Controlling idea
Building a personal learning network is a deliberate, ongoing practice - not a passive accumulation of follows and subscriptions - and professionals who treat it as a system compound their expertise faster than those who do not.

## Promise to the reader
By the end of this book, you will be able to build and maintain a personal learning network that grows your expertise systematically, keeps you current in your field, and connects you with practitioners who think differently than you do.

## What this book is NOT
This book is not about growing an audience, building a personal brand, or optimizing social media performance. Those goals are downstream of learning. This book focuses on the upstream practice of deliberate curation and connection.
