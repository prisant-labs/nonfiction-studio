## Notes

### Chapter 1: Listening Before Speaking
The passive-consumption rate cited in this chapter comes from Harold Jarche's work on personal knowledge mastery; see SRC-0003 (Jarche 2014) for the primary online resource. The legitimate peripheral participation concept draws from Hart's treatment in SRC-0001 (Hart 2015), who synthesizes the original Lave and Wenger framework for a professional audience. The listen-to-speak ratio and the network trust observations also draw from SRC-0001.

### Chapter 2: Finding Your Network
The 150-person social cognition finding references the Dunbar number, first formalized in SRC-0002 (Granovetter 1973) adjacent literature; for this book, the claim traces to SRC-0002. The curation satisfaction and source-diversity retention data come from SRC-0004 (PewResearch 2016). The weak-ties novelty claim is the central finding of SRC-0002 (Granovetter 1973). The pacing recommendation traces to SRC-0001 (Hart 2015).
