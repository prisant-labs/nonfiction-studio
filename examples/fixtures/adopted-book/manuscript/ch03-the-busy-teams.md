# The Busy Teams

The two software teams counted almost everything. They counted tasks finished each week, the
time each task spent waiting for review, and the number of times a finished task came back. Their
dashboards were full, and both teams felt that they were working hard without moving.

The first team stopped counting tasks altogether for a month. Each Friday they wrote down one
thing a user could now do that the user could not do on Monday. Some weeks the list was empty,
and the empty weeks started the most useful arguments the team had all year.

The second team kept its dashboard but added a single question to its weekly meeting: which of
these numbers would we stop collecting if nobody asked us for it? Over three months they retired
half of their measures, and nobody outside the team noticed.
