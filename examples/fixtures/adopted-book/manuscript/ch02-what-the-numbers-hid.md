# What the Numbers Hid

The repair shop had a different problem. Its owner could tell you, to the dollar, what each month
had earned. He could not tell you why a customer who had been coming for years suddenly stopped.

He tried a survey first, and almost nobody answered it. Then he tried calling the customers he
had lost, one each evening, and asked a single question: what would have made you come back? He
expected complaints about price. He heard instead about waiting, about being told a repair would
take two days and finding it still on the bench on the fifth.

The numbers had hidden this because the numbers were about money, and waiting costs the shop
nothing until the customer leaves. Once he started tracking the gap between the promised day and
the real one, the gap became the first thing he checked each morning.

Within a season the gap had narrowed, and some of the customers he had called came back. He does
not claim the calls caused it. He says only that he finally knew what to look at.
