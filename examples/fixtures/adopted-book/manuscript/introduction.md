# Introduction

Most teams measure what is easy to count. They count tickets closed, meetings held, and pages
shipped, and they treat the totals as a portrait of their work. The totals are honest, but they
answer a question nobody asked.

This book follows four small teams through a year of trying to count something harder. One was a
county library that wanted to know whether its evening classes changed anything for the people
who came. One was a repair shop that wanted to know why some customers never returned. The other
two were software teams, and they wanted to know whether their own process helped them or merely
kept them busy.

None of them found a perfect measure. Each of them found a better question, and the better
question changed what they did on Monday morning. That change is the subject of this book.
